Basis labels: **[V]** verified by a probe or a read of the DB on this host; **[D]** cited from a
first-party doc/page; **[S]** secondary source (search summary / third-party write-up), not
primary; **[A]** assumption.

Routes: **FREE-HIST** = free history exists; **FWD** = free only by recording forward from now;
**PAID**. "Held" = already in `analytics.db` or on disk.

## Verified vs assumed

- **Verified this run**: Binance vision `metrics` and `bookDepth` columns/cadence; `aggTrades` file reachable; Bybit REST and `public.bybit.com` reachable from this host; Bybit trade CSV header; Deribit option-trade `iv` back to 2018-06 and DVOL first row 2021-03-24; Coin Metrics community MVRV; `analytics.db` taker-buy and `open_interest` counts (opened read_only, no lock); heatmap JSON count and date range; Farside Cloudflare block.
- **Reused from earlier probes (not re-run)**: vision earliest keys per symbol, Binance REST 30-day caps, OKX/Bybit paging depths, WS message rates, Coinglass-free Coin Metrics metric lists.
- **Not verified**: the aggTrades/trades header inside the zips (columns taken from the binance-public-data README; I did not unzip); Binance `forceOrder` WS being a throttled snapshot (Binance docs, not re-read); floors for Deribit option history, OKX 1H candles and Bybit 1h OI (paging caps stopped the earlier probe); every ETF-flow claim other than the Farside block; Coinglass and Coin Metrics paid prices; Tardis and ExoCharts figures are web-page summaries rather than a human read of the price page.
- **Does not generalise**: the 15-min vs 1h-4h-1d coverage asymmetry (15m is BTC/ETH/SOL only) matters for any tape-derived sub-15m study.

## Contradictions to the brief's claims

1. **Bybit is NOT geo-blocked from this host.** `api.bybit.com`, `api.bytick.com`, `api.bybit.nl` and `public.bybit.com` all returned 200 today (`probe_okx_bybit.out`, `probe_bybit_only.out`, re-checked this run). The claim was true of the old Linux box at most; it should be read as "we do not use it", not "it cannot be had".
2. **"OKX publishes no taker-buy split" is true of its candles only.** The 9-field candle row has no taker column [V], but `rubik/stat/taker-volume` returns buy/sell volume per bar and `history-trades` carries `side`. Depth is the real limit: weeks to months, so it cannot backfill anything.
3. **Deribit DVOL starts 2021-03-24, not 2021-04.** A fortnight earlier; immaterial but the date is now pinned.
4. **Binance REST OI and long/short ratios are not "history"**: 30 days only. The 5-min vision `metrics` files are the history route and go to 2020-09-01 (BTC) / 2021-12-01 (most majors).
5. **Taker-buy volume is already held**, on every bar, 100% non-null, which makes a tape purchase unnecessary unless the question needs resolution below 15m or price-level (footprint) detail.
6. **MVRV is free on Coin Metrics for BTC, ETH and 7 more assets, but not SOL or AVAX**, and every liquidation/OI/funding market endpoint is 403 on the community key.
7. **Binance bookDepth is percentage-band depth, not a book.** Full L2 is paid only (Tardis).
8. **Open-interest backfill is free and not yet done**: `open_interest` starts 2026-02-28 / 2026-05-22 while vision `metrics` reaches 2020-09 / 2021-12.
