## Answer

The register holds 110 rows: 104 distinct measured items plus 6 sleeve roll-ups that restate A-rows (all 6 sleeve verdicts hold). Of the 104 items, 48 stand (43 clean, 5 amended with the direction intact after a re-test), 7 are superseded or withdrawn, 37 are priced, descriptive, design or still-open items with no verdict to flip, 7 carry an un-re-run caveat whose bias runs AGAINST the filed verdict, and 5 carry a caveat of unknown direction that was never re-run. The 7 are A08 (10 live T6 volume_suppress flips), A15 (exit-policy composite A/B), A25 (reference-level proximity), A27 (structural touch-decay), A28 (H9 REVERSE), A31 (H8 positive cells) and A09 (the pooled direction-axis claim). No sleeve verdict is exposed; the deploy core's one open gap is that no forward OOS exists.
